Straight Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you discover roles before LinkedIn?

Companies post on their own ATS or career site first — it's free and immediate. Job boards like LinkedIn charge posting fees and have editorial lag. TalentRadar watches these primary sources directly, so you capture a mandate the moment it goes live — typically 24–72 hours before it appears on any job board.

Which ATS platforms are supported out of the box?

Greenhouse, Lever, Workable, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Teamio, Recruitis, and custom careers pages. For non-standard enterprise career sites, we build dedicated custom scraping adapters — included in the Scale/Enterprise plan.

What happens when a client closes a vacancy?

Our auto-archive daemon detects removed listings on the next scan and archives them automatically. Your pipeline stays current with zero manual cleanup required.

How does the 14-day free pilot work?

Track up to 200 client companies with full scanning access for 14 days. No credit card required, and there is zero automated lock-in — you decide whether to continue after the pilot ends.

Can we export our notes and data?

Yes. One-click CSV export of your complete BD pipeline, notes, and role metadata — anytime, immediately. Your data is always yours.
