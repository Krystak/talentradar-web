How TalentRadar started

From Monday-morning clicking to a radar that does it for you

In Dublin I worked at Ingenio Global, the agency run by Rob Magee, and watched experienced headhunters spend every Monday morning going through career pages and copying links into spreadsheets.

It was slow work where things were easy to miss, and it took time the consultants should have spent with candidates. So I wrote a system that checks companies’ career pages and ATS by itself.

For the next two months it ran every day in production: it watched 180 large companies and caught more than 3,400 open roles. Consultants heard about new roles on the day they were posted, days before they showed up on job boards.

Back home it was clear that hundreds of agencies across Central and Western Europe struggle with the same spreadsheets. TalentRadar is here so they don’t have to do that work.

Before you start

Frequently Asked Questions

Set up without a single call

When you sign up, paste your clients’ domains or upload their list in Excel or CSV. Within 24 hours we find their career pages and ATS and run the first scan. No calls needed.

How do you know about roles before LinkedIn and job boards?

Companies post roles first in their own ATS (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and others) or on their careers page, because it costs them nothing. Roles reach paid job boards later, often several days later. TalentRadar checks the companies’ own pages and ATS twice a day, so it picks up a new role at the next scan.

Which ATS does TalentRadar work with?

Greenhouse, Lever, Workable, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Workday, Personio, BambooHR, Teamtailor, Recruitee, Pinpoint, HireHive, Teamio/Jobs.cz, Phenom and Jibe, as well as large companies’ own career pages. We connect non-standard pages on request in the Scale / Enterprise plan.

What happens when a company closes or fills a role?

When a listing disappears from the site, the next scan (at 6:00 or 12:00 your time) notices, the one after confirms it, and TalentRadar archives the role. If the company posts it again, it comes back with its full history. Nothing to delete by hand.

How do the 14 free days work?

For 14 days you can monitor up to 200 companies and use everything in full. No credit card. The trial does not turn into a paid plan by itself: you decide whether to carry on.

Can we get the roles into our own ATS or CRM?

Yes, as a CSV file. In BD Master you export the roles in one click, with the same filters you have on screen, and import the file into your ATS. A direct connection to Bullhorn or another ATS isn’t available yet. If you don’t have an ATS, Radar + ATS runs the pipeline inside TalentRadar, and you can bring in candidates and contacts from Vincere, Loxo, Bullhorn or LinkedIn exports.
