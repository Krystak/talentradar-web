Have an MPC, a candidate you want to offer to your companies? Paste a CV or a few lines: role, seniority, technologies, location, salary. You get the open roles at your companies, best match first, and for each one why it fits and what does not, the company’s ring, how long the role has been open, whom to call, and a short anonymous pitch.

  • The profile is not stored: once you have searched, it is gone.
  • Name, e-mail, phone and links come out before anything goes to the AI.
  • You send the pitch yourself, and only with the candidate’s consent.

How TalentRadar started

From Monday-morning clicking to a radar that does it for you

In Dublin I worked at Ingenio Global, the agency run by Rob Magee, and watched experienced headhunters spend every Monday morning going through career pages and copying links into spreadsheets.

It was slow work where things were easy to miss, and it took time the consultants should have spent with candidates. So I wrote a system that checks companies’ career pages and ATS by itself.

For the next two months it ran every day in production: it watched 180 large companies and caught more than 3,400 open roles. Consultants heard about new roles on the day they were posted.

Back home it was clear that hundreds of agencies across Central and Western Europe struggle with the same spreadsheets. TalentRadar is here so they don’t have to do that work.

Before you start

Frequently Asked Questions

Set up without a single call

Send your company list in Excel or CSV, a CRM export is fine, or just paste the names. We find their career pages and ATS, run the first scan and within 48 hours send you a short overview of where they are hiring. No calls needed. If you like, we show you the search for a candidate too, in a short call.

Do I have to move my CRM or ATS?

No. A list of your companies with their websites is enough, from your CRM or a spreadsheet; other columns are optional. The new roles go back as a CSV export you import into your ATS. A direct connection to Bullhorn or another ATS isn’t available yet.

We don’t have an ATS. Is that a problem?

Not at all. TalentRadar watches companies and their roles, and needs no ATS for that. You export the roles to Excel or CSV, or work with them right in TalentRadar. If you want it, a simple record of candidates and jobs is included in the price.

Which ATS does TalentRadar work with?

Greenhouse, Lever, Workable, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Workday, Personio, BambooHR, Teamtailor, Recruitee, Pinpoint, HireHive, Teamio/Jobs.cz, Phenom and Jibe, as well as large companies’ own career pages. We connect non-standard pages on request in the Scale plan.

What happens when a company closes or fills a role?

When a listing disappears from the site, the next scan notices, the one after confirms it, and TalentRadar archives the role. If the company posts it again, it comes back with its full history. Nothing to delete by hand.

How does the free trial work?

The first 5 agencies get the first month free, then 3 months at half price. Once those places are taken, the trial is 14 days. Either way you can watch up to 150 companies and use everything in full, with no credit card. The trial does not turn into a paid plan by itself: you decide whether to carry on.

How does the 48-hour preview work?

Send your company list with the form below: active clients and prospects, the company name and its careers page are enough. We set up a trial workspace and go through their career pages. Within 48 hours Kryštof, the founder, personally sends you a short overview: how many of your companies are hiring in your niche and which roles are worth a call. In a call we then run a search for one of your candidates with you. No credit card.

Does TalentRadar send e-mails for us?

No. TalentRadar shows who to call and why, and drafts the message in your own style. You always send it yourself, from your own mailbox. No mass mailing, no automatic sequences, no open tracking.

Do you store our candidates’ profiles?

No. For a search you only paste the candidate’s profile, and once you have searched it is gone. Before anything goes to the AI we take out the name, e-mail, phone and links. Your candidates stay in your ATS.
