Zero-Friction Asynchronous Setup

Paste your target client domains or upload a CSV/Excel file during registration. Our engine maps your accounts and ATS endpoints within 24 hours. Zero configuration calls required.

How do you discover roles before LinkedIn and job boards?

Companies post directly to their ATS endpoints (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, etc.) or career portals first — it is immediate and free. Commercial job boards charge posting fees and rely on spiders with 24–96 hour indexing latency. TalentRadar monitors primary endpoints twice daily, discovering vacancies on Day Zero.

Which ATS platforms are natively supported?

Greenhouse, Lever, Workable, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Personio, BambooHR, Teamtailor, Teamio/Jobs.cz, Recruitis, and custom enterprise portals. For non-standard career pages, custom scraping adapters are included in the Scale/Enterprise plan.

What happens when a company closes or fills a vacancy?

Our Auto-Archive Daemon detects removed listings on the very next scheduled scan (06:00 or 12:00 UTC) and automatically archives them. Your active pipeline stays 100% clean with zero manual reconciliation.

How does the 14-day free pilot work?

You track up to 200 client companies with full scanning capabilities for 14 days. No credit card is required. There is no automated commitment or surprise charge — you decide whether to subscribe once you have seen the results.

Can we push roles to our existing CRM or export data?

Yes. BD Signals supports 1-click CSV exports and webhook integration for Bullhorn, Datacruit, Recruitis, or any external ATS. If you run a boutique agency without an external ATS, our built-in candidate pipeline provides complete mandate tracking.
