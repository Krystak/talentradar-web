    Who Built This

    From Manual Spreadsheet Grunt Work to Autonomous Intelligence

    During my internship in Dublin at Ingenio Global under Rob Magee, one of my main tasks was going through client career pages by hand and adding open roles into spreadsheets one by one.

    It was slow, error-prone, and a terrible waste of human capacity. Instead of spending weeks updating tables manually, I started building an automated engine to monitor client career portals directly.

    Over the next two months, the system ran in daily production — tracking 180 enterprise companies and capturing 3,400+ open roles. Consultants knew about newly opened positions on the exact day clients posted them, without having to manually check websites.

    Returning from Dublin, one thing was clear: headhunting teams across Europe are still losing dozens of hours every week doing this exact manual work. TalentRadar is the product built to solve that problem.

    Straight Answers

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How do you discover roles before LinkedIn?

    Companies post on their own ATS or career site first — it's free and immediate. Job boards like LinkedIn charge posting fees and have editorial lag. TalentRadar watches these primary sources directly, so you capture a role the moment it goes live — typically 24–72 hours before it appears on any job board.

    Which ATS platforms are supported out of the box?

    Greenhouse, Lever, Workable, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Teamio, Recruitis, and custom careers pages. For non-standard enterprise career sites, we build dedicated custom scraping adapters — included in the Scale/Enterprise plan.

    What happens when a client closes a vacancy?

    Our auto-archive daemon detects removed listings on the next scan and archives them automatically. Your pipeline stays current with zero manual cleanup required.

    How does the 14-day free pilot work?

    Track up to 200 client companies with full scanning access for 14 days. No credit card required, and there is zero automated lock-in — you decide whether to continue after the pilot ends.

    Can we export our notes and data?

    Yes. One-click CSV export of your complete BD pipeline, notes, and role metadata — anytime, immediately. Your data is always yours.
