    Precision Matching

    Trained on Your Agency's Specialization Profile

    In Settings → Specialization, you define the roles your agency places: CORE for target roles, SELECTIVE for edge opportunities, and SKIP for everything else. You can also configure core, selective, or blocked geographic markets. TalentRadar classifies every newly published role against your exact criteria so you only spend time on relevant business. When a vacancy is filled and disappears from the careers site, it auto-archives after a couple of scans.

    Who Built This

    From Manual Spreadsheet Grunt Work to Autonomous Intelligence

    During my internship in Dublin at Ingenio Global under Rob Magee, one of my main tasks was going through client career pages by hand and adding open roles into spreadsheets one by one.

    It was slow, error-prone, and a terrible waste of human capacity. Instead of spending weeks updating tables manually, I started building an automated engine to monitor client career portals directly.

    Over the next months, the system has been running in daily production across nearly 300 companies. Consultants knew about newly opened positions on the exact day clients posted them, without having to manually browse websites.

    Returning from Dublin, one thing was clear: headhunting teams across Europe are still losing dozens of hours every week doing this exact manual work. TalentRadar is the product built to solve that problem.

    Straight Answers

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How do you discover roles before LinkedIn?

    Companies post on their own ATS or career site first — it's free and immediate. Job boards like LinkedIn charge posting fees and have editorial lag. TalentRadar watches these primary sources directly, so you capture a role the moment it goes live — typically 24–72 hours before it appears on any job board.

    Which ATS platforms and career sites are supported?

    The scanner supports Greenhouse, Lever, Workable, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Recruitee, Workday, BambooHR, Personio, Teamtailor, Pinpoint, Teamio, Jobs.cz and standard career sites. For non-standard enterprise career portals, we confirm technical feasibility in advance.

    What happens when a client closes a vacancy?

    When a role disappears from the careers page, TalentRadar archives it automatically after it's been missing for a couple of scans, so your pipeline stays clean without any manual work.

    How does the 14-day free pilot work?

    You get full access with Agency Pro limits — track up to 200 client companies, up to 8 team members, and 2 daily automated scans for 14 days. No credit card is required, and there is zero automatic renewal — you decide whether to continue after the pilot.

    Can we export our data?

    Yes. One-click CSV export of your complete BD role directory and role metadata anytime from BD Master. Your data is always yours.
