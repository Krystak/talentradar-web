2. Building it yourself with AI

It works and makes sense as long as the list is small and someone owns the upkeep.

If you watch 10 to 20 straightforward career pages, ChatGPT, Claude, or a light workflow in n8n and Apify will genuinely pull new roles each morning. For a one- or two-person desk, that is practical and inexpensive.

Once the list grows past roughly 50 companies across different ATS platforms (Workday, Greenhouse, Teamtailor, Teamio), template changes quietly break scrapers every week. Maintenance stops being a one-off setup.

If there are two of you watching twenty companies, build it in ChatGPT or n8n—you’ll manage fine. If there are ten of you watching a hundred and fifty, you’ll need someone fixing broken scrapers every week.

4. When TalentRadar is not for you

We would rather point you to the right category than sell you a tool for the wrong job.

  • You need verified direct emails and phone numbers

    TalentRadar estimates the likely hiring manager from the role title and opens a one-click search on LinkedIn or Google; we don’t sell credit-based contact records. In the UK and the Netherlands, Recruit Signals or Vente AI handles that.

  • You want pre-vacancy signals before a job goes live

    Funding rounds, executive changes, and team growth signals are covered by signal platforms such as Recruit Signals or PredictLeads. TalentRadar watches live open roles, role age including 45+ days, and your contact history.

  • You want whole-market search instead of watching your own company list

    TalentRadar monitors the specific client and prospect list your agency cares about. Searching the entire unknown market by tech stack belongs to global databases such as TheirStack.

  • You need live Slack notifications or webhooks

    TalentRadar sends morning digests and exports to Excel, CSV, Bullhorn, and Vincere. Real-time Slack alerts and custom webhooks are built into Visualping Business and TheirStack.

5. Comparison FAQ

Why do you treat Teamio separately from regular job boards?

Teamio is a company ATS used across Czechia and Slovakia by a large share of employers to power their career pages and Jobs.cz listings—it is not a typical job aggregator. We haven’t found another international monitoring tool that watches local employers across Teamio, Jobs.cz, global ATS systems, and standard career websites together.

Why doesn’t TalentRadar include a database of manager emails and phone numbers?

Bulk buying and reselling personal email addresses and mobile numbers outside your existing CRM contacts runs into strict EU GDPR rules. Instead of selling contact lists, TalentRadar surfaces colleagues you already know from your CRM, estimates the likely hiring manager from the role title, and opens a one-click LinkedIn or Google search.

What does the CRM and ATS export actually support?

You upload your target company list from Excel, CSV, or a CRM export. Newly caught and open roles export as ready-to-import files for Bullhorn and Vincere, or as Excel and generic CSV for any other ATS or CRM. Your candidates stay in your own system.

If a role has been open for 45+ days, does that automatically mean the company wants an agency?

Not always. Some roles represent continuous or evergreen hiring. Still, passing 45 days usually means the role is at or past normal time-to-fill (39–45 days median in SHRM benchmarking)—giving you a concrete reason to call, not a guarantee of a fee.
