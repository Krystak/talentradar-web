Step by step

In the morning you start from your companies. When you have a candidate, you start from them.

Every role in your field, in one database

Roles are sorted newest first; scans run at 6:00 and 12:00 your time. One click narrows the list to clients or prospects, and to roles open for 45 days or more. Whom to call, and why right now

The reason is a fresh role, a role open for 45+ days, or a company you haven’t spoken to in a while. From the role’s title we estimate who most likely decides on the hire, and one click looks for them on LinkedIn or Google. The draft is in your own style, and you send it yourself.

From a candidate to open roles

A CV or a few lines about the candidate are enough. We rank the open roles at your companies by match, each with what fits, what doesn’t and whom to call. The profile isn’t stored, and the name and contact details never reach the AI. In the demo only rules do the ranking, no AI.

You send the anonymous pitch only with the candidate’s consent.

What’s real and what’s made up

Real: the companies and their open roles, read from public career pages and ATS, just as for you.

Made up: the agency, its people, contacts, candidates and jobs. In the app they carry the Demo label.
