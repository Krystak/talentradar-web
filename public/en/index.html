Three reasons to call a client

Whoever calls first has the edge

One role often goes to several agencies. The radar watches for three moments when it pays to call a client:

A new role

Your client posted a role on its own website. You know the next morning, even if they didn’t call you.

Open over 45 days

The role has been open longer than it usually takes to fill. The client may need help with it. In the public demo, 27% of 408 roles with a posting date are like this (as of 3 Nov 2026).

You haven’t spoken in a while

A company you haven’t worked with for six months or more is hiring again. It’s a good reason to get back in touch. All it takes is the date of your last contact in the list.

Every role comes with a link to the job ad and an estimate of who decides on the hire. You write and send the message yourself.

Before you start

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to change my ATS?

No. TalentRadar runs next to your ATS and nothing moves. Send your company list from Excel, CSV or your CRM. There’s no direct connection to a specific ATS yet; you download new roles as a file for Bullhorn or Vincere, for other ATS as Excel or CSV, or we send them via API and webhooks (Agency Pro and Scale). Candidates stay in your ATS.

Where do you read the roles from?

From companies’ career pages, from Jobs.cz and Teamio, and from company ATS such as Greenhouse, Lever, Workable, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Workday, Personio, BambooHR, Teamtailor, Recruitee, Pinpoint, HireHive, Phenom, Jibe, SuccessFactors, Oracle Recruiting or Eightfold. We read only pages that companies publish for job seekers themselves, and public ATS APIs. We don’t read LinkedIn: you open the search link yourself.

What if a company fills or withdraws a role?

When a posting disappears from the site, TalentRadar archives the role only once it has been missing from two scans in a row and at least two days have passed since it was last seen. A careers page that’s briefly down won’t wipe your list. A role you are working on is never archived automatically: it stays, marked as gone from the site. If the company posts the role again, it comes back with its history and notes.

Who sees our client list?

You and I see your company list, so that I can set up your radar. I don’t pass it on to anyone or use it for anything else. TalentRadar is run by its founder, Kryštof Pejša, who is also the controller of personal data. Every agency has its own separate database and sees only its own data. The app and its database run in the EU, the server in the Netherlands and the database in Ireland. Who helps us run the website and the app, what goes to the AI and how long we keep the data is set out in our privacy policy.

What happens after the pilot, or if we stop?

You can download your companies and roles to Excel or CSV at any time, or as a file for Bullhorn or Vincere. Nothing is charged automatically: if you don’t continue after the overview, the trial account ends and you pay nothing; after the pilot you pay monthly by invoice, with no commitment. I delete the agency’s data within 90 days of the end of the trial account or the subscription, or sooner on request.

What if TalentRadar shuts down?

Exporting your companies and roles works at any time, so you always have your data. If TalentRadar were to close, I would announce it at least 3 months ahead and refund any prepaid months.

How to prepare your company list

An Excel or CSV table. The company name and a link to its careers page are enough; the last contact and past placements are optional.

ABCD
1CompanyCareers pageLast contactHad placement
2Air Bankairbank.cz/volne-pozice15/03/2024yes
3Rohlik Group(company website, we try to find the careers page)
4Seznam.czkariera.seznam.cz02/11/2023no
  • Column A, Company: the company name (required).
  • Column B, Careers page: a link to the page where the company lists its open roles. If you don’t know it, enter the company website and we try to find the careers page. We can’t track a company with neither.
  • Column C, Last contact: when you last dealt with the company (optional). TalentRadar uses it to spot lapsed clients.
  • Column D, Had placement: yes or no (optional).
  • A whole CRM export is fine; we skip the other columns. Up to 150 companies, Excel (.xlsx) or .csv.

No file? Type one company per line into the field, e.g. “Air Bank; airbank.cz/volne-pozice” or “Rohlik Group; rohlik.cz”.
