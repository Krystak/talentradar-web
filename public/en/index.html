How it works

Two ways into the same roles

In the morning you start from your companies. When you have a candidate, you start from them.

New roles at all your companies, in one place

Scans run at 6:00 and 12:00 your time. Home shows the new roles by company ring, with roles open for 45 days or more and companies with a sudden jump in hiring set apart.

A reason to call with every role

The posting, the company’s history with you and the reason: a fresh role, a role open for 45+ days, or a client you haven’t spoken to in a while. AI checks the posting against your specialisation, so roles outside it stay out of your way.

Whom to call and what to say

From the posting we estimate who most likely decides on the hire and where to find them: one click to LinkedIn or Google. Your own contacts at the company come first. Then a draft in your own style, which you send yourself; it suggests e-mail only where you may send one.

Got a candidate? See who wants them

Paste a CV or a few lines about the candidate. You get the open roles at your companies, best match first, each with what fits, what doesn’t and whom to call. The profile isn’t stored, and the name and contact details never reach the AI.

You send the anonymous pitch only with the candidate’s consent.

Before you start

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to change my ATS?

No. TalentRadar runs next to it and nothing moves. A list of companies from your CRM or a spreadsheet is enough. You download the new roles as a file to import into Bullhorn or Vincere, or as a generic CSV. There’s no direct ATS connection yet.

We don’t have an ATS. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Watching companies and roles needs no ATS. If you want one, every plan includes Radar + ATS: candidates, contacts, jobs, pipeline and placements. It’s deliberately simple, built for small agencies.

Which systems does TalentRadar work with?

It reads roles from Jobs.cz and Teamio, from company ATS (Greenhouse, Lever, Workable, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Workday, Personio, BambooHR, Teamtailor, Recruitee, Pinpoint, HireHive, Phenom, Jibe, SuccessFactors, Oracle Recruiting, Eightfold) and from ordinary career pages. Out of it you get Excel, a generic CSV and an import file for Bullhorn and Vincere. We connect unusual career pages on request in the Scale plan.

What if a company fills or withdraws a role?

When the ad disappears from the site, the next scan notices, the one after confirms it, and TalentRadar archives the role. A careers page that’s briefly down won’t wipe your list. If the company posts the role again, it comes back with its history and notes.

How does the trial work, and what happens after 14 days?

The trial lasts 14 days, with no credit card, the features of the Agency Pro plan and up to 150 companies. The first 5 agencies get the first month free instead, then 3 months at half price. Three days before the end you get an e-mail. If you want to carry on, you pick a plan in the app and I send you an invoice. The trial never turns into a paid plan by itself.

Do you store our candidates?

No. When you search for a candidate you only paste the profile, and once you’ve searched it’s gone. Before anything goes to the AI we take out the name, e-mail, phone and links. Candidates are stored only if you add them yourself in Radar + ATS.

Where is the data, and who runs TalentRadar?

Every agency has its own separate database and sees only its own data. How we handle personal data is set out in our privacy policy.

Is reading career pages OK?

We read only what companies publish for job seekers themselves: public career pages and public ATS APIs. We don’t read LinkedIn automatically; you open the search link yourself.

How to prepare your company list

An Excel or CSV table. The company name and a link to its careers page are enough; the last contact and past placements are optional.

ABCD
1CompanyCareers pageLast contactHad placement
2Air Bankairbank.cz/volne-pozice15/03/2024yes
3Rohlik Group(empty, we find it)
4Seznam.czkariera.seznam.cz02/11/2023no
  • Column A, Company: the company name (required).
  • Column B, Careers page: a link to the page where the company lists its open roles (optional, we find missing ones).
  • Column C, Last contact: when you last dealt with the company (optional). TalentRadar uses it to spot lapsed clients.
  • Column D, Had placement: yes or no (optional).
  • A whole CRM export is fine; we skip the other columns. Up to 150 companies, Excel (.xlsx, .xls) or .csv.

No file? Type one company per line into the field, e.g. “Air Bank; airbank.cz/volne-pozice” or just “Rohlik Group”.
