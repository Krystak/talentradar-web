Whom to call, and why right now

Built your own AI agent?

Built it yourselves? Then you know how much work it takes.

If your own agent reliably watches the companies you need, there’s no reason to change. Building it yourself makes sense when there are two of you watching twenty companies. TalentRadar is for when there are a hundred companies and you don’t want it to rest on one person.

  • It remembers yesterday: which role a company has newly posted, which it has taken down and which has been up for more than 45 days.
  • It also reads career pages on Teamio and in companies’ ATS, which a general-purpose agent often can’t handle. It archives a role only after two scans in a row, not after one outage of the site.
  • I do the upkeep: when a company changes its website, I fix it, not your consultant.

Before you start

Frequently asked questions

Who runs TalentRadar, and who sees our data?

TalentRadar is run by its founder, Kryštof Pejša, who is also the controller of personal data. Every agency has its own separate database and sees only its own data. Your company list is seen by you and me, so that I can set up your radar. I don’t pass it on to anyone or use it for anything else. The app and its database run in the EU, the server in the Netherlands and the database in Ireland. Who helps us run the website and the app, what goes to the AI and how long we keep the data is set out in our privacy policy.

We don’t have an ATS. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Watching companies and roles needs no ATS. If you want one, every plan includes Radar + ATS: candidates, contacts, jobs, pipeline and placements. It’s deliberately simple, built for small agencies.

Where do you read the roles from?

We read only pages that companies publish for job seekers themselves, and public ATS APIs. We don’t read LinkedIn: you open the search link yourself.

Do I have to change my ATS?

No. TalentRadar runs next to it and nothing moves. A list of companies from your CRM or a spreadsheet is enough. There’s no direct connection to a specific ATS yet; you download the new roles as a file for Bullhorn, Vincere or a generic CSV, and on Agency Pro and Scale also over the API and webhooks.

Which systems does TalentRadar work with?

It reads roles from Jobs.cz and Teamio, from company ATS (Greenhouse, Lever, Workable, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Workday, Personio, BambooHR, Teamtailor, Recruitee, Pinpoint, HireHive, Phenom, Jibe, SuccessFactors, Oracle Recruiting, Eightfold) and from ordinary career pages. Out of it you get Excel, a generic CSV and an import file for Bullhorn and Vincere. You can also take the data over the API and webhooks (Agency Pro and Scale). We connect unusual career sites to order on the Scale plan.

What if a company fills or withdraws a role?

When the ad disappears from the site, TalentRadar archives the role only once two scans in a row have missed it and the second day after it was last seen has come (seen on Monday, archived on Wednesday at the earliest). A careers page that’s briefly down won’t wipe your list. A role you’re already working on stays, with a warning. If the company posts the role again, it comes back with its history and notes.

How does the trial work, and what happens after 14 days?

The trial lasts 14 days, with no credit card, the features of Agency Pro and up to 150 companies. It’s fully working within 48 hours. Whatever doesn’t connect by itself, I look up personally. Three days before the trial ends, I’ll get in touch. If you want to carry on, you pick a plan and I send you an invoice. The trial never turns into a paid plan by itself.

Do you store our candidates?

No. When you search for a candidate you only paste the profile, and once you’ve searched it’s gone. Before anything goes to the AI (Google’s Gemini via its paid API, which doesn’t use the data for training) we take out the name, e-mail, phone and links. Candidates are stored only if you add them yourself in Radar + ATS.

What happens to our data after the trial or when we leave?

You can download your companies and roles to Excel or CSV at any time, or as a file for Bullhorn or Vincere. If you don’t continue after the trial, I stop the account and you pay nothing. I delete the agency’s data within 90 days of the end of the trial or subscription, or sooner on request.

What if TalentRadar shuts down?

Exporting your companies and roles works at any time, so you always have your data. If TalentRadar were to close, I would announce it at least 3 months ahead and refund any prepaid months.

How to prepare your company list

An Excel or CSV table. The company name and a link to its careers page are enough; the last contact and past placements are optional.

ABCD
1CompanyCareers pageLast contactHad placement
2Air Bankairbank.cz/volne-pozice15/03/2024yes
3Rohlik Group(company website, we try to find the careers page)
4Seznam.czkariera.seznam.cz02/11/2023no
  • Column A, Company: the company name (required).
  • Column B, Careers page: a link to the page where the company lists its open roles. If you don’t know it, enter the company website and we try to find the careers page. We can’t track a company with neither.
  • Column C, Last contact: when you last dealt with the company (optional). TalentRadar uses it to spot lapsed clients.
  • Column D, Had placement: yes or no (optional).
  • A whole CRM export is fine; we skip the other columns. Up to 150 companies, Excel (.xlsx) or .csv.

No file? Type one company per line into the field, e.g. “Air Bank; airbank.cz/volne-pozice” or “Rohlik Group; rohlik.cz”.
