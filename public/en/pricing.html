What the trial includes

14 days, up to 150 companies, the features of Agency Pro, no credit card. Nothing is charged when it ends.

Payment and invoicing

How do I pay?

By invoice, monthly or yearly. Card payment isn’t available yet. Czech and Slovak agencies are invoiced in koruna, Irish agencies in euro.

What happens after the trial?

Three days before the end you get an e-mail. If you want to carry on, click “I want to continue” in the app, pick a plan and I send you an invoice. If not, you pay nothing.

Can I change plan or cancel?

Yes, there’s no commitment. Just write to me and I switch your plan.

How does yearly billing work?

You pay for 10 months and use 12, so 2 months are free.

Do prices include VAT?

Prices exclude VAT.
