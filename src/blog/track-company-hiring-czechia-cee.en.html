Why tracking company hiring in Czechia & CEE is uniquely fragmented

In Western European hubs such as London or Amsterdam, an agency targeting tech and corporate clients mostly encounters a handful of international ATS vendors. Across Czechia, Slovakia, Poland, and neighboring Central European markets, a single agency watchlist spans far more varied infrastructure.

Within one portfolio of priority accounts, domestic mid-market employers run on local Czech portals, multinational shared service centers use enterprise global portals, fast-growing product firms rely on cloud-native ATS tools, and industrial, banking, or retail employers maintain custom career sections directly on their corporate domains.

In TalentRadar's public demo watching 100 Czech companies as of 3 November 2026 (1,438 open roles total), 45 out of 100 companies are read directly from the company's own ATS or custom career website outside Jobs.cz and Teamio [2]. Relying solely on a single job-board alert leaves a large part of client hiring unseen.
