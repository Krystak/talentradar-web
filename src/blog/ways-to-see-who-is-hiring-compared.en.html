2. What it actually costs (including “build it yourself”)

3. When to choose what: decision guide

  • You are 1 consultant watching up to 10–15 key clients: Stick with manual Monday browser checks and a spreadsheet. Twenty minutes costs zero software budget.
  • You are 1–2 people tracking 5–20 static career pages (no Workday, Taleo, or embedded JS ATS): Use a free or low-cost page monitor (Visualping, Distill) or a simple ChatGPT / n8n flow.
  • You have an in-house developer feeding an internal warehouse or scanning unknown companies globally by tech stack: Pick a job-data API (TheirStack, PredictLeads) or build on n8n + Apify.
  • You operate in Czechia or Slovakia and primarily want market-wide job-board history and local company registry records: Evaluate Saleskit (built around Merk company intelligence and major CZ/SK job boards).
  • You watch 20 to 400+ target employers directly on their career pages, global ATS, and Teamio with zero maintenance: Use TalentRadar — {{price:solo}} (up to 50 companies), {{price:pro}} (up to 150 companies, unlimited users), {{price:scale}} (up to 400 companies), or add +50 companies packs for {{price:extra50}}.

4. Building it yourself with AI: where it works and where it breaks

If you watch 10 to 20 straightforward career pages, ChatGPT, Claude, or a light workflow in n8n and Apify will genuinely pull new roles each morning. For a one- or two-person desk, that is practical and inexpensive.

Once the list grows past roughly 30–50 companies across different ATS platforms (Workday, Greenhouse, Teamtailor, Teamio), template changes quietly break scrapers every week. Maintenance stops being a one-off setup.

If there are two of you watching twenty companies, build it in ChatGPT or n8n—you'll manage fine. If there are ten of you watching a hundred and fifty, you'll need someone fixing broken scrapers every week.

6. When TalentRadar is not for you

We would rather point you to the right category than sell you a tool for the wrong job:

  • You need verified direct emails and mobile numbers

    TalentRadar estimates the likely hiring manager from the role title and opens a one-click LinkedIn or Google search; we don't sell credit-based contact records. In the UK and the Netherlands, Recruit Signals or Vente AI focuses on that.

  • You want pre-vacancy signals before a job goes live

    Funding rounds, C-level moves, and headcount velocity belong to signal platforms such as Recruit Signals, PredictLeads, or Saleskit in CZ/SK. TalentRadar tracks live open roles, posting age including 45+ days, and your contact history.

  • You want market-wide discovery or raw APIs instead of your own company list

    TalentRadar monitors the specific client and target list your agency cares about. Searching unknown companies globally by tech stack is better served by TheirStack, or Saleskit for CZ/SK job boards and registries.

  • You need instant Slack notifications or custom webhooks per page diff

    TalentRadar runs twice daily (06:00 and 12:00), emails a weekly digest, exports to Excel, CSV, Bullhorn, and Vincere, and connects via MCP. Instant webhook or Slack alerts on raw URL changes belong in Visualping Business or TheirStack.

7. Comparison FAQ

Why do you treat Teamio separately from regular job boards?

Teamio is a company ATS used across Czechia and Slovakia by a large share of employers to power their career pages and Jobs.cz listings—it is not a typical job aggregator. We haven't found another tool that monitors local employers across Teamio, Jobs.cz, international ATS systems, and standard career websites together.

Why doesn't TalentRadar include a database of manager emails and phone numbers?

Bulk buying and reselling personal email addresses and mobile numbers outside your existing CRM contacts runs into strict EU GDPR rules. Instead of selling contact lists, TalentRadar surfaces colleagues you already know from your CRM, estimates the likely hiring manager from the role title, and opens a one-click LinkedIn or Google search.

How do company list upload and CRM / ATS exports work?

You upload your target company list from Excel (.xlsx), CSV, or a CRM export on every plan including Solo Scout. Newly caught and open roles export as ready-to-import files for Bullhorn and Vincere, or as Excel and generic CSV for any other ATS or CRM. Every plan also connects to Claude and ChatGPT via MCP. Candidates stay in your own system.

If a role has been open for 45+ days, does that automatically mean the company wants an agency?

Not always. Some roles represent continuous or evergreen hiring. Still, passing 45 days usually means the role is at or past normal time-to-fill (39–45 days median in SHRM benchmarking)—giving you a concrete reason to call, not a guarantee of a fee.

When does it pay off to switch from a DIY AI script or page monitor to a purpose-built tool?

While 1 or 2 people watch up to 20 straightforward career pages, a DIY script or generic monitor costs roughly €6–€32/month (CZK 150–775) plus 1–2 hours of monthly upkeep. Once your watchlist grows past 20–30 companies across different ATS platforms (Workday, Greenhouse, Teamtailor, Teamio) for a larger team, fixing broken templates takes an estimated 4–10 hours a month and a ready-made radar becomes cheaper in both time and money.
