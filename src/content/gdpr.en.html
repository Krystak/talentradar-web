Privacy Policy

Effective from 15 September 2026

1. Who is the data controller?

The controller of your personal data is Kryštof Pejša, who runs TalentRadar.
E-mail: krystof@talentradar.eu

2. What data do I process and why?

When you submit the trial sign-up form on this website, I process only what you fill in yourself:

  • Full name – so I know how to address you,
  • Work e-mail – to reply to you and send your trial access,
  • Company name – to know who I am setting the trial up for,
  • Client list or career page links (including an uploaded file) – to load your companies into the trial.

To protect the form against spam and abuse, I also record the IP address and browser information of each submission.

Visit statistics: to see how many people visit the website and how fast it loads, I use Cloudflare Web Analytics. It does not use cookies or any other storage in your browser and does not track you across websites. It only records anonymous page-view data such as the page visited, the referring site, country, browser type and load time (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Purpose and legal basis: setting up your trial and replying to your request (steps taken at your request before entering into a contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and protecting the form against abuse (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). I do not use your data for unsolicited marketing and I do not sell or pass it on to anyone else.

3. How long do I keep it?

I keep the contact details from your request only as long as needed to handle our communication and your trial, and no longer than 6 months after you submit the form, unless we agree to work together. An uploaded client file is deleted automatically after 90 days.

4. Who has access to it?

Only I (Kryštof Pejša) have access to your data. To run the website and the form, I use the following service providers, who process data on my behalf in line with GDPR:

  • Cloudflare, Inc. – website hosting, form storage, spam protection and visit statistics,
  • Sendinblue SAS (Brevo) – delivery of e-mail notifications and confirmations,
  • Slack Technologies, LLC – internal notification about a new request.

5. What are your rights?

Under GDPR you have the right to:

  • know what data about you I process,
  • have inaccurate data corrected,
  • have all your data erased (the right to be forgotten),
  • object to the processing,
  • lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority – in the Czech Republic, the Office for Personal Data Protection (uoou.gov.cz).

To exercise any of these rights, just e-mail me at krystof@talentradar.eu and I will act on it without undue delay.
